Community Guidelines

This community exists to help you learn faster and go further — together. A few ground rules keep it that way:

1. Be kind and constructive

Disagreement is fine; disrespect isn't. Critique ideas and work, not people. Assume good intent, and give feedback the way you'd want to receive it.

2. Keep it relevant

This is a space for learning — questions about lessons, course feedback, career advice, and sharing your progress all belong here. Off-topic promotion or unrelated content doesn't.

3. No spam or self-promotion

Don't use posts or comments to advertise unrelated products, services, or other platforms. Sharing your own work as a student (a finished project, a client win) is welcome and encouraged.

4. No harassment, hate speech, or abuse

Any content that harasses, threatens, or demeans another person or group will be removed, and repeat behavior can result in losing access to the community.

5. Respect privacy

Don't share other people's personal information, private messages, or payment details here.

6. Moderation

Admins and instructors can remove posts or comments that break these guidelines. If you see something that shouldn't be here, use your judgment and avoid engaging — reporting tools are on the way.
